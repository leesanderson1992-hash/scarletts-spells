# Controlled Production Proof Rule

For an explicitly authorised controlled Production proof, treat the named
proof endpoint as one continuous task: reproduce, add a focused regression,
repair one bounded defect slice, test, commit, deploy, then resume the fresh
proof from its start. Do not stop at a deploy, login, intermediate UI
milestone, or individual smoke assertion.

Use only disposable child fixtures, with one fixture for each named micro
skill. Controlled incorrect-spelling submissions may be inserted from approved
dictionary content and marked `verified_misspelling` with a controlled source
reference. Create the ADLE queue item through the controlled Production path,
generate the assignment, complete the live lesson manually, and verify the
persisted completion, attempt, reflection, and reward records.

Browser interruption recovery is part of the proof, not a terminal state:
reclaim or open a tab, re-authenticate the disposable account if necessary,
restore the recorded assignment, and continue from the last verified activity
without asking for permission. A proof may end only after its named Finish
action, reload/retry, and verifier checks pass; or after unrecoverable required
Production access or a genuine product decision. Never end for deploy
readiness, login, tab loss, individual activity success, or progress
reporting.

This controlled proof does not simulate or certify the ordinary parent-review
journey. It deliberately bypasses child submission, parent return, child
correction, parent classification of a persistent misspelling, finalised issue,
and Golden Nugget creation. Therefore, no Gold event is expected from the
controlled rows.

Preserve the existing safety boundaries: one bounded defect slice per commit
and deployment; no rollout, broad migration, or account changes outside the
disposable fixtures without explicit approval. Keep credentials and raw
authentic spelling evidence private.
