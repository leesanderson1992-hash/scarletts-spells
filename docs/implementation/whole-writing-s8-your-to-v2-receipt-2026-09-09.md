All cases remain in the same declared construction and the full recall
denominator. New runtime scope is explicitly narrower than general grammar:
complete bounded clauses and enumerated lexical classes are required. Unknown
vocabulary, embedded/unfinished clauses, gerunds and unresolved categories
abstain. Broad V1 adjacency heuristics are not automatically inherited by V2;
V1 itself remains available unchanged under its persisted identities.

curly and modifier-letter apostrophes, rejects substring reanchoring, retains

unparsed punctuation and ellipses. Each independent family manifest includes
fingerprints for its own source, the shared helper and unchanged V1
normalisation dependency. Changing the helper requires new matching pins for
each affected release.

Counterexamples precede acceptance of each extension: distance versus
