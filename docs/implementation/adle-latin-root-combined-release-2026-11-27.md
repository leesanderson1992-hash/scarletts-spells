# Combined Latin-root lessons — 27 November 2026

This operational receipt extends the earlier PORT release. The ADLE composer,
evidence, review and reward contracts remain authoritative.

Lessons select five or six words in total from one or two relevant Latin-root
families. Six is preferred when reviewed content is available. Two-family
lessons support three plus three and four plus two; a five-word lesson uses
three plus two. Each family retains a separate tree page. Selection preserves
authentic target lineage and uses reviewed family members for transfer practice.

A family needs two complete reviewed members to join a combined release;
it does not need five words of its own. The release builder discovers eligible
Latin families from the dictionary. It does not treat a root's illustrative
example list as reviewed word facts. Families without sufficient reviewed words
remain unavailable until their content is completed and published.

The initial combined Production pool contains PORT (transport, portable,
import, export, porter) and VIS (visible, vision). Production's existing
`in + visible` analysis and Base-family membership for invisible are preserved.
The existing Production dictionary word vision received its missing reviewed
seeing sense, British pronunciation, sentence and `vis + ion` analysis. Meaning
and pronunciation were checked against [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/vision).
No learner needs or assignments were created in Production.

reload and an identical Finish retry. It retained 20 completed items/events,
12 independent attempts, two authentic taught words, one reflection and one

reload. A further three-plus-three proof with reviewed vision content also passed Finish, reload, identical retry and the same 20-item/12-independent-attempt readbacks.
