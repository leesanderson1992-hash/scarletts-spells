# Family Tree Detective review gate

Family Tree Detective is implemented as a candidate Word Lab activity for the eight weak-vowel micro-skills. The runtime reads `content/secret-life-of-vowels/detective-approved.json`, which intentionally has no rounds while the owner reviews the sheet. The development preview at `/dev/adle/family-tree-detective` uses candidate target bindings and existing owner-approved British word clips; it creates no learner evidence.

The Google Sheet's **Family Tree Detective review** tab contains one row for each of the 210 existing word–micro-skill pairs. Column J is the existing full target span; K–M are the proposed single vowel tile, zero-based letter index and masked word. N distinguishes structural candidates from blockers. H is the chosen stable pronunciation ID; O–Q identify the approved audio clip and its approval scope. R–U are editorial family candidates, not governed family membership. V–Y are reserved for the verified authority, members, relative audio and any justified spelling or morphology explanation. Z and AA hold separate Part 1 and Detective decisions.

The five-tile challenge cannot present a target outside A/E/I/O/U or an unreviewed occurrence. `bicycle` currently targets `y`; `brochure` has no confirmed single vowel occurrence. Both remain blocked for this activity. Rows marked Excluded or Deassign pending are retained for audit and are not assignment candidates. `human → humanity` and `music → musical` are design examples, not current assignments in this eight-skill review inventory.

At publication, the selected word must match the governed assignment, exact target span, word sense, pronunciation ID, source fingerprint and approved word-recording hash. The family adapter reads effective members from an activated Base Family authority. It does not create phoneme-change mappings or infer that a relative has a clearer vowel. A changed or withdrawn family is omitted from future Detective snapshots while the approved missing-vowel challenge may remain available. Existing snapshots keep their frozen content.

The first vowel choice, optional family clues and second choice are guided evidence. `clearer_sound` is the child's observation. Meaningful-part and same-spelling clue controls require reviewed evidence before they are offered. `no_helpful_clue` excludes other clue selections for that relative. These choices and activity completion do not project an independent spelling success; later answer-hidden production remains governed by the existing proficiency rules.

Before live activation, reconcile the sheet with the current governed assignment and Base Family authority, validate source fingerprints and clips, publish approved rows through the content workflow, and run staging completion and repeat-Finish checks. The candidate recipe and preview are not route activation.
