# Family Tree Detective review gate

Family Tree Detective is implemented as a candidate Word Lab activity for the eight weak-vowel micro-skills. The runtime reads `content/secret-life-of-vowels/detective-approved.json`, which intentionally has no rounds while the owner reviews the sheet. The development preview at `/dev/adle/family-tree-detective` uses candidate target bindings and existing owner-approved British word clips; it creates no learner evidence.

The Google Sheet's **Family Tree Detective review** tab contains one row for each of the 210 existing word–micro-skill pairs. Column J is the existing full target span; K–M are the proposed single vowel tile, zero-based letter index and masked word. H is the chosen stable pronunciation ID; Q–S identify the word audio clip and its approval scope. T is an editorial family candidate, U shows the existing family name, and V holds the owner's family code to keep. Z records a verified published family ID only when the target is an eligible member. AA–AC hold reviewed relatives, relative audio and any justified spelling or morphology explanation. AD and AE hold separate Part 1 and Detective decisions.

On the 20 October 2026 readback, 26 rows had a family choice in V. Twenty-two target memberships were verified against a published Base Family authority and their IDs were recorded in Z. *Baker*, *jumper* and *actually* have approved morphology but are not eligible members of their chosen published family; they need governed family-member review and publication. *Photographer* has two codes in V (`photo_base_family | graph_base_family`), so no single family can be bound. Its original target annotation stays intact. The other 184 rows have no V choice. None of these family choices alone approves a pronunciation, word audio, clue, or Detective round; AD and AE were still unapproved at that readback.

The five-tile challenge cannot present a target outside A/E/I/O/U or an unreviewed occurrence. `bicycle` currently targets `y`; `brochure` has no confirmed single vowel occurrence. Both remain blocked for this activity. Rows marked Excluded or Deassign pending are retained for audit and are not assignment candidates. `human → humanity` and `music → musical` are design examples, not current assignments in this eight-skill review inventory.

At publication, the selected word must match the governed assignment, exact target span, word sense, pronunciation ID, source fingerprint and approved word-recording hash. The family adapter accepts the exact owner-selected key from a pinned, published v1 or v2 Base Family authority only when the target is an eligible member. It does not create phoneme-change mappings or infer that a relative has a clearer vowel. A changed or withdrawn family is omitted from future Detective snapshots while the approved missing-vowel challenge may remain available. Existing snapshots keep their frozen content.

The first vowel choice, optional family clues and second choice are guided evidence. `clearer_sound` is the child's observation. Meaningful-part and same-spelling clue controls require reviewed evidence before they are offered. `no_helpful_clue` excludes other clue selections for that relative. These choices and activity completion do not project an independent spelling success; later answer-hidden production remains governed by the existing proficiency rules.

Before live activation, reconcile the sheet with the current governed assignment and Base Family authority, validate source fingerprints and clips, publish approved rows through the content workflow, and run staging completion and repeat-Finish checks. The candidate recipe and preview are not route activation.
